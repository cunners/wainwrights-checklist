Wainwrights Checklist

Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

Wainwrights Checklist doesn't collect any of your data. There's no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your logbook stays on your iPhone.

Your logbook

The summits you log, with their dates, ratings, notes, companions and weather, are stored only on your iPhone. If iCloud Backup is turned on, they're included in your iPhone's backup, which Apple stores under Apple's privacy policy. The developer can't see your logbook.

Photos

You add photos with the iPhone's photo picker, so the app only ever receives the photos you choose. It doesn't ask for access to your photo library and can't see the rest of it. Each photo you add is copied into the app at a smaller size and kept on your iPhone with your logbook. Your photo library is never changed.

Location

If you allow it, your location is used only to show where you are on the Map tab, while you're using the app. It's never stored or sent anywhere.

Weather

When you tap Fetch weather on a log, the fell's map coordinates and the summit date are sent to Apple's weather service (WeatherKit) to look up that day's weather. Nothing that identifies you is sent. Weather is provided by Apple Weather under Apple's privacy policy.

Links to other services

  • what3words: tapping a fell's what3words address opens the what3words website or app, where what3words' privacy policy applies.
  • Email Support: opens your email app. The developer receives only what you choose to send, and uses it only to reply to you.

Export and import

Exporting makes a file of your logs (and, if you choose, your photos) only when you ask, and it goes only where you send it. Importing reads only the file you pick.

Deleting your data

Delete a log in the app to remove it and its photo copies. Deleting the app removes everything it has stored on your iPhone. Copies in older iCloud backups are managed by Apple in your iCloud settings.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions? Email craigncunningham@me.com.
